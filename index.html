OpenNews

江湖多跑腿，正史得一本 — in a jianghu where rumours fly everywhere, OpenNews keeps one true record: no rumours, only the best version, after everyone's fact-check.

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

News produced strictly by the Wikipedia method, not the feed method: a story is one canonical living page that converges toward truth as facts land — updated, corrected, with its history visible.

Every story opens with a lead that stands alone, and carries an infobox card: who / what / when / where / status / sources.

Every claim is sourced or labelled （已證實 / 報道中 / 未能核實）; corrections are part of the article, never silent edits. Opinion exists only in labelled sections, never blended into the report.

A rumour may appear only labelled as a rumour-under-checking （查證中）, never dressed as news. The crowd is not the audience of the verification layer — the crowd IS the verification layer.

Dogfood № 1 is the Dawn 日報 (daily paper): the day it runs at Wikipedia grade daily, OpenNews's engine is proven.

Related surface: Global News Shorts (globalnewsshorts.dawnstudio.app) is a separate live video surface — the founding brief asks whether it is the video form of the same canon: one canon, two surfaces.

End Goal

Every day's news at Wikipedia's information grade and summarisation power: one canonical, inspectable version of what actually happened — this world's 正史 (true record) — with trust that is inspectable, not asserted. News as a public good, free forever.
